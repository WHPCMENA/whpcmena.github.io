مرحباWelcome to WHPC MENA

Women in high-performance computing across the Middle East & North Africa

We are the official Women in HPC affiliate for the region. We connect women working in supercomputing, advanced computing and related fields, make their work visible, and help the next generation into the field.

What we do4 units
U1ConnectA regional network of women in HPC across universities, research centres and industry.
U2MentorGuidance and career support from people who have built careers in the field.
U3ShowcaseTalks and stories that make women role models in computing visible.
U4AdvocateInclusive practices and a stronger HPC ecosystem in the region.

An affiliate of Women in HPC

Women in HPC works worldwide to improve equity, diversity and inclusion in high-performance computing. Since December 2025, WHPC MENA has been its official affiliate for the Middle East and North Africa.
