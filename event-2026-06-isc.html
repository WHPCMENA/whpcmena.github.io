Events / ISC High Performance 2026

Past event

ISC High Performance 2026

WHPC MENA spoke at two sessions and connected with the global Women in HPC network at ISC High Performance 2026 in Hamburg.

  • Overview
  • Upcoming
  • Past events

Mennatallah Samier Saleh and Safae Bourhnane represented WHPC MENA at ISC High Performance 2026, Europe's major conference for high-performance computing.

Our sessions

Finding Your People in HPC: A WHPC Community Meet-Up

14:15–15:00 (Berlin time) · Hall H, Community Stage, ground floor

Mennatallah Samier Saleh presented WHPC MENA's story, activities and next steps.

Tech Talks by Women in HPC

19:00–20:30 (Berlin time) · Hall H, Booth L01, ground floor

  • Mennatallah Samier Saleh: WHPC MENA
  • Safae Bourhnane: Adaptive Mixed Precision for Energy-Efficient High-Performance Computing

Highlights

  • Shared the WHPC MENA story with the international HPC community.
  • Attended deep-dive workshops.
  • Exchanged experiences with Women in HPC leaders from other chapters and affiliates.
  • Met women in HPC from the MENA region and around the world.

We came home with new insights and a stronger commitment to building the future of HPC in our region. Thank you to everyone we met and to every leader who shared their expertise with us.

Dates
22–26 June 2026
Location
Hamburg, Germany
Speakers
Mennatallah Samier Saleh and Safae Bourhnane
