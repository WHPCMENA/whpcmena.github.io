About WHPC MENA

WHPC MENA (Women in High Performance Computing – Middle East & North Africa) is the official regional affiliate of the global Women in HPC initiative.

  • Overview
  • Mission
  • Our team
  • Partners

We are dedicated to closing the gender gap in high-performance computing (HPC) across the Middle East and North Africa. We support and amplify women in HPC, advanced computing and related fields through networking, knowledge sharing, mentorship and outreach, shaped for the MENA context.

WHPC MENA is a collaboration between four founding institutions in four countries: NYU Abu Dhabi in the UAE, MoroccoHPC with UM6P in Morocco, Kuwait University, and KAUST in Saudi Arabia. Our community now reaches seven countries.

We welcome everyone who is interested in HPC and in building a more inclusive and diverse computing ecosystem in the region.

Our story

How we got here

  1. June 2025
    The idea

    WHPC MENA begins with a conversation at ISC 2025 in Hamburg.

  2. 7 Nov 2025
    Application

    We submit our formal affiliate application to Women in HPC.

  3. 5 Dec 2025
    Approval

    Women in HPC approves WHPC MENA as its affiliate for the Middle East and North Africa.

  4. 7 Jan 2026
    Launch

    Our visual identity and LinkedIn page go live.

  5. Jan 2026
    First conference

    We represent the region at SCA/HPCAsia 2026 in Osaka.

  6. 21 Apr 2026
    Kick-off webinar

    Her HPC Journey, our first public event.

  7. Jun 2026
    ISC 2026

    We deliver sessions at ISC High Performance in Hamburg.
