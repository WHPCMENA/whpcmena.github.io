Your Work Won't Speak for Itself: Building Visibility as a Woman in Science and Tech

A webinar on communicating the impact of your work and building your professional visibility, with Dr Enas Qudeimat of NYU Abu Dhabi.

  • Overview
  • Upcoming
  • Past events

We often hear that hard work speaks for itself. But does it really?

Doing great work is only part of the journey. Making our work visible, our voices heard and our contributions recognised matters just as much. It's about communicating the impact of what we do and creating opportunities for the recognition it deserves.

This is the conversation we're bringing to the WHPC MENA community in our first webinar since the kick-off and our ISC presentations.

Speaker

EQ

Dr Enas Qudeimat

Head of Research Management and Outreach, Center for Genomics and Systems Biology (CGSB), NYU Abu Dhabi. Drawing on her experience in research leadership and science communication, she will share practical insights into building professional visibility and communicating the value of our work.

What we'll explore

  • How to communicate your work and its impact with confidence
  • How to build your own professional visibility plan
  • How to use LinkedIn and institutional platforms effectively
  • How AI tools can help you tell your professional story

Who it's for

Students, early-career researchers, established scientists and technology professionals who want to strengthen their professional presence.

At WHPC MENA, we believe in creating spaces where women can share experiences, develop new skills and gain the recognition their contributions deserve. We can't wait to see you there!
