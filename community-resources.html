Community

  • Women in HPC

    The global organisation we are affiliated with: membership, mentoring, webinars and events.

    womeninhpc.org
  • ACM SIGHPC

    The ACM special interest group on high-performance computing, with student programmes and awards.

    sighpc.org

Learning

  • HPC Carpentry

    Free lessons that teach the basics of using a cluster, the command line and job schedulers.

    hpc-carpentry.org
  • TOP500

    The twice-yearly ranking of the world's most powerful supercomputers, with system details.

    top500.org

Conferences

  • ISC High Performance

    Europe's major HPC conference, held each year in Hamburg, Germany.

    isc-hpc.com
  • SupercomputingAsia (SCA)

    The Asia-Pacific HPC conference series, held jointly with HPCAsia in 2026.

    sc-asia.org
  • SC, the International Conference for High Performance Computing

    The largest annual HPC conference, held each November in the United States.

    supercomputing.org
